Mad Rat Cafe

ABOUT THE GAME

Mad Rat Cafe is a top-down roguelite about a post-apocalyptic cafe where you cook orders and shoot your way through mutant waves. Fix broken kitchen machines and stack wild upgrades to survive every shift.

IN DEVELOPMENT / PC

Announcement trailer · also on YouTube

Gameplay

You are working in a last-standing cafe, armed with a spatula in one hand and a gun in the other. Find a way around dealing with orders, mutants, and breaking machines at the same time!

01

COOK AND SHOOT

Prepare dishes, play machine minigames and fix machines that break mid-shift, all while gunning down the mutant waves flooding your cafe.

02

PICK YOUR KITCHEN

At every location, you draft a new machine. Arrange your kitchen setup, unlock new dishes, and use active machine skills that power up both cooking and combat.

03

SPEND YOUR EARNINGS

Complete orders to earn cash and bonuses, then spend it in the shop on upgrades that power up both your cooking and your shooting.

04

MAKE BROKEN BUILDS

Combine upgrades into broken builds that can turn your whole cafe into an unstoppable bullet-hell machine!
